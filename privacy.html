Privacy policy

Inventory Management is used only by its owner. It requests the Google Drive scope drive.file, which lets it see and change only the files it creates itself. It uses that access solely to upload its own encrypted database backups and a spreadsheet copy of its purchases to the owner's Drive, and to delete its own old backups.

It reads no other Drive files, collects no data about anyone else, and shares nothing with third parties. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Contact: cjsellers1999@gmail.com
